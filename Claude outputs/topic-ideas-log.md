# Power Grab TX — Topic Ideas Log

Ideas pitched in scheduled runs. Check here before pitching so nothing repeats.

## 2026-09-24

1. **The Pause Button: How Texas Cities Can (and Can't) Stop a Data Center** (evergreen guide / local impact) — Explains Texas's one-shot moratorium law (Ch. 212 Subch. E, 120 days + 180 extension, HB 2559 supermajority), Dallas's "by right" zoning gap, and why counties have even less power; uses Dallas, Lubbock, Denton, Fort Worth as case studies.
2. **The 90% Ask: What Texas Pays to Land a Data Center** (follow the money) — Sales-tax exemption cost jumped from ~$29M (FY2025) to $1B+/yr, heading to ~$1.8B by 2030, while only 20 of 138 facilities have been audited; AWS's 90%/30-year Hood County rebate ask shows local abatements stack on top. Abbott and Hinojosa both now promise to end incentives.
3. **The Jobs Cliff: 7,500 Hard Hats, 50 Badges** (local impact / community) — The construction-vs-permanent jobs gap (Google/Crusoe Armstrong County: ~7,500 workers, ~50 permanent jobs), 6 of 20 audited tax-break projects failed job minimums, Texas can't independently count data center jobs, and the Abilene rent spike from worker housing.

## 2026-09-29

1. **Off the Grid, Off the Hook? The Texas Data Centers Outside ERCOT's Rules** (follow the money / ratepayers) — El Paso (WECC) and the Panhandle (SPP) sit outside ERCOT's interconnection audit, so ratepayer protection there comes down to utility-by-utility fights: EPE's $499–552M, 366-MW McCloud gas plant for Meta (judges: approve only with lifetime hold-harmless; PUC order by Dec. 7) and Xcel's new large-load tariff.
2. **Closed Loop, Open Questions: How AI Data Centers Actually Use Texas Water** (tech explainer / evergreen) — Explains evaporative vs. closed-loop vs. free-air cooling and the water-for-power tradeoff, then tests industry claims (Crusoe's "domestic use only," Skybox's "less than five households") against a state that still can't count: <30% survey response, Paxton's 18-county probe, Denton writing low-water cooling into code.
3. **Powered Land: Why an Energized Plug Is Texas's Most Valuable Real Estate** (follow the money) — With ERCOT's Batch Zero paused (474 GW queue) and TCEQ permits frozen, sites that already have power are repricing: Hut 8 paid ~3x the floor for bankrupt Poolin's Pyote/Tarbush, New Era's 20-yr Vistra gas PPA turned a site into "permitted powered land," and the $22B, 6.3-GW Encinal plan bets on building your own grid.

## 2026-10-01

1. **The Data Center Ballot: Where Texas Candidates Stand Before You Vote** (evergreen voter guide / SEO) — Side-by-side of Abbott vs. Hinojosa, Paxton vs. Talarico and Patrick vs. Goodwin on tax breaks, local approval, water, grid costs and the permit freeze; the real dividing line is who gets final say on a project, with registration closing Oct. 5 and early voting Oct. 19; plus local ballot items like Granbury's recall.
2. **Bring Your Own Power: How Texas Data Centers Are Building Private Grids** (tech explainer) — How islanded/behind-the-meter campuses work (gas engines vs. simple-cycle turbines vs. combined cycle, trigeneration chillers, diesel backup), the "minor source" permitting split challenged in the Vantage/VoltaGrid suit, the emissions math, and the contradiction of Abbott demanding data centers bring their own power while TCEQ can't permit it.
3. **The Forever Hum: Why Texas Can't Turn Down a Data Center** (local impact / community) — Texas counties can't pass noise ordinances, so neighbors from Mitchell Bend to Abilene to Granbury have tried incorporation, lawsuits and recalls; examines what noise, light and 24/7 construction actually do, and what Abbott's promised "setbacks and noise reduction" would need from the 2027 Legislature.

## 2026-10-05

1. **The 249.99 Problem: How Texas Power Plants Slip Under Federal Review** (tech/regulatory explainer) — Explains major vs. minor source and the 250-ton PSD line, using the Lufkin emails (TCEQ reviewer asked Amp Z's developer to get PM under 249.99 tpy; one-hour cut, minor-source approval) and the Vantage/VoltaGrid "separate entity" lawsuit; what PSD would have required (BACT, modeling, public hearing) and what TCEQ's Oct. 19 update should answer. Builds on, but is distinct from, 10/01's "Bring Your Own Power."
2. **The Water Tower Deal: How Data Centers Are Buying Goodwill in Rural Texas** (follow the money / community) — $343M+ in local giving (QTS's $3–5M Turkey water tower, Lancium's Childress pipeline, Rowan's $38M in Temple), Amazon's $1B pledge and NDA reversal, and the Lone Star Infrastructure Coalition; asks what towns get in writing, who keeps the leverage when a company is the water utility, and why 57% of Texans still say no.
3. **Homes Per Megawatt: Do Texas Data Centers Pay Their Way?** (evergreen guide / SEO) — How a data center is taxed (sales-tax exemption vs. property tax, Ch. 312 abatements, phase-in by % complete), Fort Worth's 8,378-homes figure and the 14x TTARA claim, and a checklist for reading a local tax-abatement deal. Flip side of 9/24's "The 90% Ask" (what Texas gives up vs. what it gets back).
